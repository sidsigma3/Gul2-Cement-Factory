The process

Six stages, two plants, one QA chain.

01 / 06

Aggregate batching

Computer-controlled batching with calibrated load cells. OPC 53-grade cement, washed river sand, 6–12 mm graded aggregate. Mix designs locked per product family, tagged by batch number on every truck.

02 / 06

Vibration moulding

Hydraulic press + dual-frequency vibration on European-grade block machines. Output: 1,600 pavers per hour per line, three lines running concurrent product runs across the two plants.

03 / 06

Steam curing

Controlled-humidity curing chambers run a 24-hour primary cure, followed by 28-day stack cure on the yard. Each batch is photographed and logged at release for traceability.

04 / 06

Finishing & shot-blast

In-line shot-blasting, polishing, sandblasting and exposed-aggregate finishing. Bespoke surface treatments offered for architectural orders — pigment-matched mixes and bespoke profiles.

05 / 06

QA lab testing

In-house lab equipped with CTM 2000 kN cube-compression machine, digital balance, abrasion tester and water-absorption tanks. Daily sample test logs; per-dispatch conformance certificates.

06 / 06

Logistics & dispatch

Owned 24-truck fleet plus partner pool covers 18 states. Pallet-wrapped shrink-bound dispatch. GPS-tracked deliveries with ETA notification on every consignment.
