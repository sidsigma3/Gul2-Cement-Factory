ISO 9001:2015 · BIS · IS 15658 · IS 12118

Tested before it leaves the yard.

Every consignment is sampled, tested in our in-house lab, and dispatched with a certificate of conformance. Below: the standards we hold, the tests we run, and the documentation you get with every truck.

Certifications

Held and renewed.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management System

Awarded 2012, renewed every three years. Full audit of process documentation, batch traceability, non-conformance handling and continuous improvement programmes. Both plants in scope.

BIS · IS 15658:2006

Paver Blocks · Standard Mark

BIS Standard Mark licensed for our paver block range. Authorised to mark conforming product with the ISI logo. Routine BIS surveillance and sample testing.

BIS · IS 12118

Kerb Stones · Conformance

Kerb stones cast and tested to IS 12118 dimensional and compressive-strength requirements. Conformance certificate accompanies every consignment to PWD and NHAI contractors.

IS 13801 — Cement tiles

Chequered / Plain tiles

Cement tiles cast to IS 13801. Per-batch tests cover dimensions, water absorption and wet transverse strength. Per-dispatch certificate.

IS 458 — Concrete pipes

NP2 / NP3 / NP4 classes

Concrete pipes (where supplied) tested to IS 458 hydrostatic, three-edge bearing and strength requirements. Class NP2 to NP4 depending on the project specification.

EN 1433 — Drains

Load classes A15–F900

Drainage channels cast to EN 1433 load-class specifications with corresponding grating systems. Compliance tested per project class A15 (pedestrian) to F900 (heavy industrial).

Documentation

What ships with your order.

  • Invoice + delivery challanStandard tax invoice with GSTIN, e-way bill and signed delivery challan.
  • Test certificatePer-batch results for compressive strength, water absorption and dimensional check against the relevant IS code.
  • Mill test certificate of cementOPC 53-grade MTC for the cement batch used.
  • BIS Standard MarkWhere applicable (paver blocks, kerbs), each unit carries the ISI logo and the licence number.
  • Installation guideFor pavers, kerbs and tile floors — laying pattern, joint width, sand-bed depth, edge restraint and curing instructions.
  • Custom-order documentsFor bespoke castings: CAD-approved drawing, mix-design summary, sample-casting photo log and pigment-match certificate.
