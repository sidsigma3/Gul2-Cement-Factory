Where our concrete works.

Tarini Enterprises supplies to civil contractors, municipal authorities, landscape designers and industrial buyers. Below: the sectors we serve, the products that move into each, and the kind of project where we add the most value.

/ 01

Real estate & housing

Driveways, compound walls, garden paving and footpath tiles for residential townships, gated communities and individual builders. Pavers in M-30/M-35, boundary systems at 6–10 ft, plus cement pots and planters for landscaping.

Pavers Boundary walls Tiles & planters
/ 02

Roads & highways

Barrier kerbs, mountable kerbs, drainage channels and tactile tiles supplied to NHAI and state PWD contractors. We've delivered to L&T, Dilip Buildcon and several regional civil contractors across the eastern corridor.

Barrier kerbs Median dividers Tactile paving
/ 03

Municipal infrastructure

Pavers, kerbs, drainage and benches for urban local bodies, smart-city missions and PMAY projects. Conformance to BIS standards, on-time tendered supply, certificates with every dispatch — the procurement chain ULB officers ask for.

ULB tenders Smart city PMAY supply
/ 04

Landscape & gardens

Architectural planters in 12 SKUs, stepping pavers, garden edging, water-feature elements and bespoke castings for landscape architects working on hotels, residences and urban parks.

GFRC planters Garden edging Stepping pavers
/ 05

Commercial buildings

Façade panels, lobby planters, entrance plaza systems for IT parks, malls, hotels and offices. Worked with architecture firms on bespoke moulds for branded entrance plazas.

Plaza paving Lobby planters Façade fins
/ 06

Industrial parks

Heavy-duty pavers (M-50, 100 mm thick), compound wall systems, drain channels with load class D and F gratings — built for the wheel loads of SEZ logistics yards and manufacturing campuses.

Heavy-duty pavers Compound walls Class D/F drains
/ 07

Public spaces

Concrete benches, planters, tactile and chequered tiles for parks, riverfronts, transit plazas and station platforms — products that have to survive monsoons, heatwaves and high foot-traffic for a decade.

Park benches Platform tiles Tactile floors
/ 08

Custom & architectural

Architect-specified precast: façade fins, signage bases, art-piece moulds, brand-installation castings. CAD to sample in 14 days; pigment-matched mixes; exposed-aggregate and sandblast finishes available.

Bespoke moulds Public art Pigment-matched
