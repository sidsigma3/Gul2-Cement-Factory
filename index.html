01 · The Company

Built for builders. Specified by engineers. Trusted by municipalities.

For over two decades, Tarini Enterprises has supplied precast concrete products to the people who shape India's cities, roads, and public spaces — from civil contractors building bypasses in tier-2 cities to municipal authorities procuring kerbs, channels and pavers by the truckload.

We don't trade. We manufacture — at our own plants, with our own moulds, under our own quality lab. Every block we ship carries a batch number, a casting date, and a certificate of conformance to the relevant IS code.

06 · Manufacturing

From batching plant to truck bed.

Six stages, two plants, one QA lab. Every product passes through the same chain — engineered for repeatability at industrial scale.

01 / 06

Aggregate batching

Computer-controlled batching with calibrated load cells. OPC 53-grade cement, washed river sand, 6–12 mm graded aggregate. Mix designs locked per product family.

02 / 06

Vibration moulding

Hydraulic press + dual-frequency vibration on European-grade block machines. Output: 1,600 pavers per hour per line, three lines running concurrent product runs.

03 / 06

Steam curing

Controlled-humidity curing chambers. 24-hour primary cure, 28-day stack cure on yard. Tracked by batch, photographed at release.

04 / 06

Finishing & shot-blast

In-line shot-blasting, polishing, sandblasting and exposed-aggregate finishing. Bespoke surface treatments for architectural orders.

05 / 06

QA testing

In-house lab runs cube compression (CTM 2000 kN), water absorption, density and abrasion tests. Sample certificate per dispatch.

06 / 06

Logistics & dispatch

Owned 24-truck fleet plus partner pool. Pallet-wrapped shrink-bound dispatch. GPS-tracked deliveries, ETA notification on every consignment.
