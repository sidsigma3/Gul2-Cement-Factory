Est. 2003 · Khurda · Odisha

Two decades of precast, one philosophy: make it ourselves.

Tarini Enterprises was founded in 2003 with a single block-making press in Khurda. We've spent the twenty-two years since growing carefully — adding a second plant, a QA lab, a custom-mould workshop, and an owned fleet — without ever outsourcing what we put our name on.

The company story

A precast company, not a trading house.

Most of our competition in eastern India buys finished blocks from job-work units and resells them with a margin. We don't. Every product we ship is cast on a press we own, in a yard we control, by a workforce on our rolls. That decision costs us in flexibility — but it's the only way to take responsibility for what reaches the site.

It also lets us do the things that matter most to civil engineers: lock a mix design, repeat it at scale, test the output, and dispatch certificates with every truck. Buyers know what they're getting before the first block is laid.

Our customers are civil contractors, landscape architects, municipal authorities and industrial buyers — the people for whom dimensional accuracy, on-time supply and conformance to IS code are not optional.

Milestones

Twenty-two years, six expansions.

2003

Founded in Khurda

Single hydraulic press, six workers, supplying solid blocks to local home-builders within a 60-km radius.

2008

First PWD supply contract

Awarded a kerb supply tender for state highway resurfacing — the year we started casting M-30 grade products to IS code.

2012

ISO 9001 certification

Full quality-management system in place. In-house QA lab commissioned with cube-press, digital balance and curing tanks.

2016

Sambalpur plant commissioned

Second plant on 4.2 acres opened to serve western Odisha and Chhattisgarh civil-contractor demand for pavers and kerbs.

2020

BIS licence for IS 15658

BIS Standard Mark for paver blocks awarded after audit. Authorised to mark blocks with the ISI logo on dispatch.

2024

Custom mould workshop

In-house mould-engineering setup added to take architect-specified profiles from CAD to sample casting in 14 days.
